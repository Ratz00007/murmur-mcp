# Pro Plan Pricing — effective Q1

The Pro plan moves from $29 to $41 per seat per month, a 40% increase. The increase funds three things: a new observability layer, a 99.99% uptime SLA, and on-prem support later this year.

## What stays the same

- Existing customers keep current pricing for six months (until the end of Q2).
- The free tier keeps unlimited personal projects.
- Data exports remain free forever.
- Support response times do not change.

## What changes

- Pro: $29 → $41 per seat/month (existing customers until end of Q2).
- Free tier: 3 projects → 1 project.
- Free tier API rate limits: 5,000 → 1,000 requests/hour.
- Federation and on-prem are Pro-only at GA.

## Migration

Existing projects above the free-tier limits must be archived or upgraded by end of Q2. The migration guide walks through archiving, exporting and downgrading. Student and nonprofit accounts get a 12-month amnesty window.
