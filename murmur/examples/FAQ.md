# Pricing FAQ

**Q: Why now?**
A: Reliability. The platform doubled uptime this year and shipped a major API overhaul. The new pricing funds the observability layer and the 99.99% SLA customers asked for.

**Q: I'm an existing customer — when does this hit me?**
A: Not until end of Q2. You keep $29/seat until then. After that, the new price applies to your next cycle.

**Q: What happens to my third project on the free tier?**
A: It stays accessible read-only for 90 days, then archives automatically. Export any time — data exports are free forever.

**Q: Is there an amnesty program?**
A: Yes. Students and nonprofits keep full Pro features for 12 months at no cost.

**Q: Are rate limits negotiable?**
A: The 1,000/hour free-tier limit is permanent. Pro keeps 50,000/hour.

**Q: Will on-prem be available on the free tier?**
A: No. On-prem is Pro-only at GA, scheduled later this year.

**Q: Can I lock in the old price for a year?**
A: No annual locks are offered for legacy pricing.
